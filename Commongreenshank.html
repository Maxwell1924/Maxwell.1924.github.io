INFORMATION - COMMONGREENSHANK
  • The Common Greenshank is a medium-sized wading bird known for its long greenish legs and slightly upturned bill.
  • It is often found in wetlands, mudflats, marshes, estuaries, and shallow coastal waters.
  • Common Greenshanks feed on insects, small fish, crustaceans, worms, and other aquatic creatures.
  • They are strong migratory birds, traveling long distances between breeding and wintering grounds.
  • Their loud, ringing calls are often heard when they are disturbed or flying overhead.
  • Common Greenshanks are active foragers and usually search for food by walking steadily through shallow water.
