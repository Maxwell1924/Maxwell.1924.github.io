INFORMATION - LITTLEEGRET
  • Little Egrets mainly feed on fish, frogs, insects, and small aquatic animals.
  • It is commonly found near rivers, wetlands, lakes, marshes, and coastal shores.
  • The Little Egret is a small white heron recognized for its elegant appearance and black beak.
  • Their long black legs and bright yellow feet help them move carefully through shallow water.
  • During breeding season, they grow long decorative feathers called plumes on their head and back.
  • Little Egrets are skilled hunters and often use quick movements or foot-stirring techniques to catch prey.
