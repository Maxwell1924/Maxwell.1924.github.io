INFORMATION - PURPLEHERON
  • The Purple Heron is a tall wading bird known for its slender neck and reddish-purple feathers.
  • It is commonly found in wetlands, reed beds, marshes, and near slow-moving rivers or lakes.
  • Purple Herons feed mainly on fish, frogs, insects, and small reptiles.
  • They are patient hunters and often stand still for long periods before striking at prey.
  • Their long legs and sharp beak help them move through shallow water and catch food efficiently.
  • Purple Herons are shy birds and usually hide among tall reeds to avoid predators and disturbance.
