INFORMATION - SANDERLING
  • Sanderlings are small shorebirds commonly found running along sandy beaches near waves.
  • They are known for their fast movements as they chase food along the shoreline.
  • Sanderlings migrate long distances, traveling between Arctic breeding grounds and warmer coastal regions.
  • During winter, their feathers are mostly pale gray and white, helping them blend into beach environments.
  • They can be found in environments ranging from forests to cities.
  • Unlike many birds, Sanderlings do not have a hind toe, which helps them move quickly across sand.
