INFORMATION - BLACK WINGED STILT
  • The Black-winged Stilt is a graceful wading bird known for its extremely long pink legs.
  • It is commonly found near wetlands, lagoons, salt pans, and shallow lakes.
  • Black-winged Stilts feed on insects, small fish, crustaceans, and aquatic invertebrates.
  • Their striking black-and-white feathers help them stand out in wetland habitats.
  • These birds are excellent walkers and often move slowly through shallow water while searching for food.
  • During breeding season, Black-winged Stilts become very protective of their nests and chicks.
