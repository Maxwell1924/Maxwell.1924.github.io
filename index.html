DAILY FACTS
  • Birds are vertebrates with lightweight bones that help many species fly.
  • They lay eggs and often build nests to protect their young.
  • Different species have unique beaks adapted to their diet.
  • Birds communicate through songs, calls, and body movements.
  • They can be found in environments ranging from forests to cities.
  • Owls can rotate their heads up to 270 degrees to better observe their surroundings.
  • Hummingbirds are the only birds capable of flying backward.
  • Some birds migrate thousands of miles each year to find food and warmer climates.

Birds are warm-blooded animals with feathers and beaks, found in habitats all over the world. Many can fly, and they play an important role in nature by spreading seeds, pollinating plants, and controlling insects.
