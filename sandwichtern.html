INFORMATION - SANDWICHTERN
  • The Sandwich Tern is a coastal seabird recognized by its black crest and long pointed bill with a yellow tip.
  • It is commonly found near beaches, estuaries, harbors, and coastal islands.
  • Sandwich Terns mainly feed on small fish, which they catch by diving into the water from the air.
  • They are highly social birds and often nest in large colonies with other seabirds.
  • Their wings are long and slender, allowing them to glide smoothly and travel great distances.
  • During breeding season, Sandwich Terns perform aerial courtship displays and offer fish to their mates.
